## Rozeta Labs Sales Deck — LLM Build Brief

**13 slides. 16:9 format. Visual theme: warm greige background (#D9D5CC), bold black typography, electric blue (#4169E1) border and accent, lime green (#C5E24A) as pop color, charcoal (#2D2D2D) for dark elements. Every slide has a thin blue border on all four edges. Footer: "Rozeta Labs" left, slide number right.**

### SLIDE 1 — COVER

**What goes on the slide:**

Top left, small spaced caps label in blue:
`REVENUE RECOVERY`

Center left, massive black headline in two lines:
`You're losing money`
`between the lead and the job.`

Blue underline rule beneath the headline.

Below the underline, one line of body text in dark gray:
`We find exactly where. We fix it. You keep the revenue.`

Bottom left, blue filled rectangle button:
`LET'S FIND YOUR LEAK`

Right side decorative elements: stacked ellipses (wireframe), lime green blob shape, charcoal 4-pointed star. These are geometric, not illustrative.

**What does NOT go on this slide:** No logo parade. No "about us." No agenda slide. No tagline. No subheading explaining what Rozeta Labs is.

### SLIDE 8 — OBJECTIONS

**What goes on the slide:**

Top left, small spaced caps label in blue:
`BEFORE YOU ASK`

Left side headline in large bold black:
`What we hear most`
`at this point.`

Blue underline rule.

Five objection blocks stacked vertically. Each block has two parts: the objection in a dark red or charcoal pill/label on the left, and the answer in body text to the right or below.

**Objection 1:**
Label: `"We already have a CRM."`
Answer: `We build on top of it. The problem isn't the tool — it's that nobody uses it consistently. We automate the consistency.`

**Objection 2:**
Label: `"We've tried agencies before."`
Answer: `Agencies drive traffic. We fix what happens after. Different category entirely.`

**Objection 3:**
Label: `"How fast will we see results?"`
Answer: `Most clients see measurable recovery in the first 30 days. First system is live in 14 days.`

**Objection 4:**
Label: `"What if it doesn't work?"`
Answer: `See the next slide.`

**Objection 5:**
Label: `"Our team won't adopt new technology."`
Answer: `Your team doesn't use it. It runs without them. Nothing changes in their daily process.`

**What does NOT go on this slide:** No defensive language. No "but" or "however." No apology. Present framing: "Before we get to pricing, here are the five things we hear most often at this point in the conversation."
