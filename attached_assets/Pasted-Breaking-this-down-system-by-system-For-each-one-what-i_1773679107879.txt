System 01 — 60-Second Response Engine
Kills: Speed-to-Lead Failure
The problem it solves: Lead comes in. Nobody responds for 20 minutes. Competitor calls back in 4. Job is gone.
How it works:
Lead trigger fires — web form submission, missed call, Google LSA inquiry, Facebook lead ad, any inbound channel
Instantly sends a personalized SMS to the lead — uses their name, references the service they inquired about, sets an expectation ("Someone from our team will call you in the next few minutes")
Simultaneously notifies the right person internally — dispatcher, sales rep, or owner — via SMS or Slack with the lead details
If no internal response within X minutes, escalates to the next person in the chain
Logs everything in the CRM automatically — lead source, timestamp, response time, outcome
Tools: GoHighLevel or HubSpot + Twilio + n8n/Make. If they're using Jobber or ServiceTitan, webhook into that.
Output: Zero unacknowledged leads. Response time under 60 seconds on every channel. Internal accountability on who responded and when.
Revenue recovery mechanism: Capture the jobs you're already paying to generate but losing to response time. Industry data says responding within 60 seconds vs 5 minutes increases close rate by 400%.

System 02 — Dead Estimate Reactivation Sequence
Kills: Estimate Graveyard
The problem it solves: Quote goes out. Prospect goes quiet. Nobody follows up because it's awkward, time-consuming, or just falls off the radar. $50K–$150K sitting in a spreadsheet going nowhere.
How it works:
Pull all open estimates older than 7 days with no response — from their CRM, Jobber, ServiceTitan, or a spreadsheet if that's what they're using
Enroll every open estimate into a timed follow-up sequence:
Day 7: "Just checking in" SMS — casual, low pressure
Day 14: Value-add email — "We noticed you haven't moved forward yet, wanted to share what other [roofing/HVAC/etc] customers in your area have been dealing with this season"
Day 21: Urgency trigger — "We have availability opening up next week, wanted to give you first right of refusal before we fill it"
Day 30: Final ask — "Should I close this out or is this still something you're considering?"
Any response at any stage pulls them out of the sequence and notifies the sales rep immediately
Non-responders after Day 30 move to a long-term nurture (quarterly touch)
Tools: GoHighLevel or Smartlead for the sequence + n8n to pull open estimates from CRM + AI layer to personalize each message based on job type, estimate amount, and prior communication
Output: Systematic follow-up on 100% of open estimates with zero manual effort. Most clients recover 3–8 jobs in the first 30 days just from quotes that were already written.
Revenue recovery mechanism: You already did the hard work of getting the lead and writing the quote. This recovers money that's already half-earned.

System 03 — No-Show Recovery Protocol
Kills: No-Show & Cancellation Bleed
The problem it solves: Appointment booked. Crew shows up or gets ready. Customer isn't there, doesn't answer, or cancels same-day. Slot lost, drive time wasted, revenue gone.
How it works:
Appointment confirmation sequence fires automatically at booking:
48 hours before: confirmation SMS with one-tap confirm/reschedule option
24 hours before: reminder with job details and crew ETA window
2 hours before: final heads-up with crew name and arrival time
If customer no-shows or cancels same-day:
Immediate recovery SMS: "We missed you today — we have availability [X] and [Y], which works better for you?"
If no response in 2 hours: second attempt via different channel (email or call task assigned to rep)
If no response in 24 hours: enters a 3-touch re-engagement sequence over the following week
Cancellations trigger an open slot alert internally — gives dispatcher the window to backfill with another job from the waitlist
Tools: GoHighLevel or Jobber native + Twilio for SMS + n8n for the logic layer
Output: Dramatic reduction in cold no-shows. Most operators recover 30–40% of no-shows that previously just disappeared. Backfill system keeps crews productive.
Revenue recovery mechanism: Every recovered no-show is a job you already had. No new lead cost. No new sales effort. Just recovered capacity.

System 04 — Owner Liberation System
Kills: Owner as Bottleneck
The problem it solves: Every decision, approval, question, and exception goes through the owner. Business can't scale past his personal bandwidth. He works 60-hour weeks and still feels behind.
How it works — this is the most custom of the five, built in phases:
Phase 1 — Bottleneck Audit Map every recurring decision the owner makes. Categorize by frequency and complexity. Identify which ones can be systematized with clear rules vs which ones genuinely require judgment.
Phase 2 — Decision Automation Build decision trees for the high-frequency, lower-complexity decisions:
Quote approval thresholds — anything under $X auto-approved, anything over $X flags for review
Job dispatch logic — crew assignment based on availability, proximity, skill set, and job type
Supplier reorder triggers — materials inventory hits threshold, PO drafts automatically for owner approval
Customer status updates — job status changes automatically notify customer via SMS without office staff involvement
Phase 3 — AI Assistant Deployment Build a company-specific AI assistant trained on their SOPs, pricing logic, supplier contacts, and job history. Crew and office staff ask it questions instead of the owner:
"What's our markup on TPO roofing?"
"What's the warranty on the shingles we used for the Johnson job?"
"What do I tell a customer who's asking about a competitor's lower quote?"
Phase 4 — Reporting Replacement Owner currently gets information by asking people or being present. Replace with automated daily/weekly briefings — job status, revenue collected, estimates outstanding, crew performance — delivered to his phone every morning.
Tools: n8n + OpenAI for the AI assistant + Notion or Airtable as knowledge base + Slack or SMS for delivery
Output: Owner removes himself from 50–70% of day-to-day operational decisions within 90 days. Business runs during vacations, sick days, and busy seasons without him as the single point of failure.
Revenue recovery mechanism: Not direct revenue recovery — capacity recovery. Every hour the owner gets back is an hour he can spend on sales, strategy, or just being home by 5pm.

System 05 — Repeat Revenue Reactivation Campaign
Kills: Zero Retention Engine
This is actually three sub-systems running together:
Sub-system A — Post-Job Review Capture Sequence
Job marked complete in CRM/Jobber/ServiceTitan
2 hours later: personalized SMS to customer — "Thanks for having us out today. If we did good work, we'd really appreciate a quick Google review — it means everything to a small business. Here's the link: [direct Google review link]"
48 hours later if no review: second softer ask via email
7 days later if no review: final ask — "No worries if it's not your thing, just wanted to make sure you were happy with the work. Anything we could have done better?"
Sub-system B — Referral Ask Protocol
Fires 7 days after job completion to customers who left a 4–5 star review or confirmed satisfaction
SMS: "We're really glad you were happy with the work. The biggest way you can help us is by referring a neighbor or friend — if they book with us, we'll take $X off your next service."
Referral tracked in CRM. Reward triggered automatically when referred job is booked.
Sub-system C — Seasonal Reactivation Campaign
Every past customer gets segmented by job type and service date
AI generates personalized outreach tied to seasonal relevance:
Roofing customer from 18 months ago → pre-storm season inspection offer
HVAC customer from last summer → pre-winter tune-up campaign
Gutter customer → fall cleaning reminder
Sent via SMS and email on a rolling calendar — never a blast, always contextually timed
Tools: GoHighLevel + n8n + OpenAI for personalization layer + CRM integration for job history and segmentation
Output: 3–5x increase in Google review capture rate. Measurable referral pipeline from existing customers. Repeat revenue from past customers who've gone quiet.
Revenue recovery mechanism: Your most profitable customer is one you already have. Zero acquisition cost. Maximum trust. This turns your completed job list into a recurring revenue asset.
