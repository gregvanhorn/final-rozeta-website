### AI Agents Track

Instead of naming specific agents, describe the *function archetype* each card represents. Four archetypes that cover most SMB use cases:

---

**Card 1: Cost & Margin Intelligence**
*Any industry where materials, labor, or supplier pricing affect profitability*

Watches your numbers so you don't have to. Monitors costs across suppliers, job types, or vendors — normalizes the data, spots drift, and surfaces the signal before it hits your P&L. You find out about a margin problem before the invoice arrives, not after the job closes.

---

**Card 2: Pre-Decision Risk Scoring**
*Any business making bets — on jobs, deals, hires, or projects*

Builds a fingerprint for every outcome you care about, then scores new decisions against it. Before you commit resources, you get a probability — not a gut feeling. The agent learns what your best outcomes actually looked like, and tells you whether this one looks like those.

---

**Card 3: Institutional Memory**
*Any team that runs on calls, emails, documents, and conversations*

Your business generates knowledge constantly. Most of it disappears. This agent ingests it — recordings, emails, notes, SOPs, CRM data — and makes it queryable. Any team member can ask what was decided, agreed, or discovered, and get an answer in seconds instead of digging through inboxes.

---

**Card 4: Pipeline & Relationship Health**
*Any business with a sales cycle, client base, or ongoing relationships*

Reads the signals buried in your communication patterns — response times, engagement, activity gaps, tone shifts — and builds a live health score for every relationship or opportunity. Tells you which ones need attention before they go quiet.

---

### Custom Applications Track

Three archetype cards covering the main *types* of custom apps SMBs need:

---

**Card 1: Estimation & Proposal Tools**
*Built around how you actually price and sell*

A custom front-end for generating quotes, proposals, or scopes — wired into your real numbers. Not a template with blanks. Pulls live costs, applies your margin logic, and outputs a professional deliverable in under two minutes. Your process, automated.

---

**Card 2: Operations & Scheduling Dashboards**
*Built for the people running the work, not reading about it*

A real-time view of whatever your team needs to coordinate — jobs, crews, assets, inventory, capacity. Drag-and-drop assignment. Automated notifications. Live status. Built specifically around how your operation runs, not how a software company imagined it might.

---

**Card 3: Client & Stakeholder Portals**
*Built to eliminate the check-in call*

A branded, login-gated environment where clients, partners, or stakeholders see exactly what you want them to see — status, documents, approvals, messages. Keeps communication organized and on your terms. Eliminates the "where are we on this?" thread entirely.

---

**Why this works:**

- Every card describes a *type of problem* any SMB could have — not a specific solution for a specific vertical
- The prospect reads the card and thinks "we have that problem" rather than "we're not in home services"
- You still sound concrete and capable — not vague or hand-wavy
- The card structure stays identical, just the content swaps
